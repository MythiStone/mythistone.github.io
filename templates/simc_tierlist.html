Methodology
  • Fights are simulated as Patchwerk at 1, 3, 5 and 8 targets. Fight length is tuned per target count: single-target runs 3 minutes to reflect a dungeon boss while the multi-target fights run 1 minute to reflect a Trash pulls.
  • Popular is the spec's most-popular Mythic+ gear set. The same items, enchants, gems and talent loadout shown on its spec page.
  • SimC BIS is the highest DPS full gear set. It is found by evaluating thousands of gear combinations built from items equipped in high Mythic+ runs but using the same popular talents.
  • Top 50 is the loadout of the top 50 verified players: the most-common equipped item in each slot worn with their most-common talent loadout.
  • Each row is ranked by its best gear set.
  • Tanks are ranked separately and only by damage output. Survivability is not simulated.
  • Click on each bar to view the gear and talents used in that simulation.
  • Numbers are raw damage. Funnel, priority-target cleave, burst windows and utility, much of what decides real Mythic+ strength, are not represented. Treat this as throughput potential, not a meta ranking.
